ATLAS / METHOD

Knowledge is a tool. Freedom is the aim.

From Zhuangzi and cognitive sovereignty to actual creation: a method that can be tested, revised and dismantled.

Eight propositions

01

Knowledge is not cognition

Remembering answers is not understanding questions. Knowledge may be limitless; life and attention are not. Learning is not an endless duty to be certified. Choose questions, examine premises, test reasoning.

Editorial synthesis of personal discussions and notes; Chinese originals in the thought index.

02

Truth needs no crown

Scientific effectiveness grants no unlimited authority. Evidence, counterexamples and reproducibility matter; credentials, titles, journals and procedure cannot replace argument. Coherence is not truth; local success is not universal validity.

Editorial synthesis of personal discussions and notes; Chinese originals in the thought index.

03

From studying and exams to speaking and doing

My mathematical training at SUSTech is part of my experience, not the authority behind my work. I question paths centered on examinations, credentials and papers: when procedures consume time without adding information and evaluation replaces discovery, education and academia must be open to correction.

04

Scaffolding, not a shrine

In my reading of Zhuangzi, thought is removable scaffolding. Use what works, revise what fails, remove what is redundant. Zhuangzi is no exception. His debates with Huizi suggest thought experiments: test premises through concrete situations and counterexamples, not grand theories.

05

Language is a key, not the landscape

Fasting the mind and sitting in forgetfulness are experiences not reached through verbal debate alone. Cook Ding is not an argument against learning: skill grows through action, feedback and sensitivity to structure. Knowledge can serve action without becoming its mandatory license.

06

Qiwulun: examine the standard first

My reading does not flatten all things into equality. It asks how judgments about things arise and who sets the standard. Views have limits; facts can still refute claims. Opposition to authority must not become another unquestionable authority.

Editorial synthesis of personal discussions and notes; Chinese originals in the thought index.

07

Subtraction before addition

Use a tool, find a problem, improve the tool, use it again. Every new concept, rule or check should change a decision; complexity without useful feedback should be removed. Mathematics, AI, code and philosophy are tools. Work and reality test their limits.

08

Let knowledge flow freely

Share knowledge and work openly so people can judge freely. Charge for tested, maintained, executable methods and tools. Payment does not make a method true; anyone may test, adapt or reject it.

Thought is not a new authority

These are personal readings and practical principles, not the only interpretation of Zhuangzi or scientific theorems. Creative freedom does not override others’ rights; works, arguments and tools remain accountable to evidence, feedback and responsibility.
